# CTF Write-ups

Sanitized write-ups from hands-on CTF rooms (TryHackMe, with HackTheBox and
picoCTF to come). Each one focuses on the concepts, the method, and — where it
applies — **how a SOC would detect the attack**, since my focus is blue team / SOC.

Per each platform's Terms of Service, these write-ups contain **no flags, no found
credentials, and no step-by-step solutions**. The full details stay in my private
notes; what's here is the reasoning and the defensive takeaway.
